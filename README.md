# Smart-school-bus-tracking-ai
*Smart School Bus Tracking System – Making Every School Journey Safer and Smarter*

Imagine a situation where parents have no idea where their child's school bus is, how long it will take to arrive, or whether their child has safely reached school.

Our project aims to solve this problem.

Smart School Bus Tracking System is an integrated web-based platform that provides real-time bus tracking, estimated arrival times, instant notifications, student pickup and drop monitoring, and centralized transportation management.

It connects parents, students, drivers, and school administrators through one intelligent platform.

Our vision is simple: No more uncertainty, unnecessary waiting, or communication gaps in school transportation.

We are not just tracking buses; we are building a smarter, safer, and more transparent school transportation ecosystem.
